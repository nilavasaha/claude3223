# Pitch ideas + WhatsApp intros (Oct 2026)

Fill in [Your name] and [Agency]. Anything in [CONFIRM ...] must be checked with the client before sending.

## How these are written (journalist feedback: "pitch not clear, sounds like ChatGPT")

- Short. 4 to 6 lines max on WhatsApp. One story idea per message, not three.
- First line says who you are. Second line says why you're messaging *them* (a piece they wrote).
- Say the story in one plain sentence. If you can't, the pitch isn't ready.
- One clear ask at the end: a call, a sample, a visit, or "should I send details?"
- No "hope you're doing well", "delighted", "unique", "revolutionising", "thought leader", no bullet points, no em dashes.
- Send it from your own number, edit a word or two per person, and don't forward the same text to 20 people in one go.
